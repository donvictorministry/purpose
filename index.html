Preface

The quest for purpose transcends cultures, generations, and circumstances, uniting humanity in a shared pursuit of meaning. As a theologian, I have witnessed countless individuals grapple with questions like, “Why am I here?” and “What is God’s plan for my life?” The Bible offers a timeless answer: our purpose is rooted in God’s sovereign design, revealed through faith in Jesus Christ. This book, Finding Purpose in Life: A Christian Perspective, presents five biblical lessons to guide you toward discovering and living God’s unique plan for you.

Drawing from Scripture, each chapter explores a facet of purpose—creation, trials, gifts, eternity, and daily obedience—offering exegetical insights and practical steps. Written for believers and seekers alike, this work reflects my commitment to equipping the global church with transformative truth. My prayer is that these pages inspire you to trust God’s plan, embrace your calling, and live for His glory.

As you read, may you encounter the Holy Spirit’s guidance, finding clarity and courage to walk in purpose. Let us embark on this journey together, anchored in the promise of Jeremiah 29:11: “For I know the plans I have for you, declares the Lord, plans to prosper you and not to harm you, plans to give you hope and a future.”
